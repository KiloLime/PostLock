A tourist threw rocks at a endangered seal and bragged he could just pay the fines.
But the internet ruined his life in ways no one could have expected and almost murdered him too.
This 40 year old man was throwing rocks at Laney, an endangered Hawaiian monk seal, beloved by the local community.
A local woman caught it all on video and immediately confronted him, but he didn't stop throwing rocks and didn't apologize.
He even said, "I'm rich, I can pay the fines."
Instantly the video spread, and internet investigators started digging.
They posted his full name, phone number, home address, and the name of his business online.
His phone blew up with death threats within the hour.
The business's Google listing got thousands of one-star reviews from outraged strangers.
Locals on the island started a deadly manhunt.
They shared his photo everywhere and told each other to find him before he got on a plane.
Groups searched the beaches, the bars, and every rental property they could check.
When they found his rental, they blocked his car in and started banging on the windows.
He tried to call for a ride, but no driver would pick up after seeing who he was.
He had to leave the island the next morning, but he couldn't get a direct flight.
Stuck at the airport for hours, he was recognized immediately.
They shouted at him and took pictures until security escorted him to a back room.
He finally got a flight out, and his vacation ended early.
He’s now also under a federal investigation and is looking at a fine of up to $50,000.
