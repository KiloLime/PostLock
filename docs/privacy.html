Privacy Policy

PostLock is self-hosted, open-source software. This policy describes how the software handles data.

What PostLock processes

  • Narration scripts you provide are sent to the generative-AI provider you configure (e.g. DeepSeek) solely to generate titles, captions and hashtags.
  • Videos you upload are sent to the social platform you choose (TikTok / YouTube) using that platform's official APIs and your own account credentials.
  • Credentials (API keys, access tokens) are stored only in your local .env file on your own machine. PostLock never collects or transmits them anywhere else.

What PostLock does NOT do

  • No account, analytics, telemetry or advertising services.
  • No third-party data collection, cookies or tracking.
  • No cloud service operated by the PostLock project — everything runs on your machine.
