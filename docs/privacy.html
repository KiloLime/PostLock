Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

1. Overview

PostLock ("the Software") is an open-source, self-hosted publishing tool for short-form content creators. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Software processes, why, and what your rights are. Because the Software runs on your own machine, the vast majority of your data never leaves your computer except through the third-party services you choose to connect.

2. Data controller

The Software is developed and maintained by the PostLock project as open-source software. There is no centralized service, database, or "PostLock cloud": the project does not operate servers that collect your data. For questions about this policy, open an issue on the GitHub repository.

3. Information the Software processes

  • Narration scripts. The scripts you provide are sent to the generative-AI provider you configure (e.g. DeepSeek) solely to generate titles, captions and hashtags.
  • Videos. The video files you choose are uploaded to the social platform you select (TikTok and/or YouTube) through that platform's official APIs, using your own account and credentials.
  • Credentials. Your API keys, client secrets and access tokens are stored only in the local .env file on your own machine and are never transmitted to the PostLock project.
  • Account identifiers. When you connect a TikTok account, the Software receives the identifiers returned by TikTok's OAuth flow (such as your open ID) to authenticate uploads on your behalf.

4. How the information is used

Information is processed for a single purpose: delivering the features you request — generating captions from your script and delivering your video to your accounts for review. The Software does not perform analytics, profiling, advertising or automated decision-making about you.

5. Third parties

  • Generative-AI provider (e.g. DeepSeek): receives your script text to generate captions, under that provider's own terms and privacy policy.
  • TikTok: receives your video and metadata through the official Content Posting API under TikTok's privacy policy and terms.
  • YouTube: receives your video and metadata through the Data API v3 under Google's privacy policy.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web-server logs (such as IP addresses) in accordance with GitHub's privacy statement. This website sets no cookies and runs no third-party trackers.
  • GitHub hosts the public source repository and issue tracker; content you post there is public.

6. Cookies and tracking

The PostLock website sets no cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The Software itself does not use cookies.

7. Data retention

Because data resides on your machine and in the accounts you connect, you control retention: delete .env to remove stored credentials, and delete drafts or uploads in your TikTok/YouTube accounts. The project retains no data of its own. Data you share with third-party services (for example, a script sent to a caption provider) is subject to that provider's retention practices.

8. Security

The Software stores credentials only in the local .env file. You are responsible for protecting your machine and your API credentials. The project does not collect, store or transmit your data, and therefore operates no infrastructure whose compromise could expose your information.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and comparable laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Since the Software is self-hosted, you can exercise these rights directly: delete .env to erase stored credentials, and remove any uploaded drafts or videos in your platform accounts. For data held by third-party services, contact those services.

10. Children's privacy

The Software is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International transfers

Data may be processed by the third-party services you choose (caption providers, TikTok, YouTube, GitHub) in countries other than your own, under their respective privacy frameworks. By using the Software you understand this.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page with its effective date.

13. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, open an issue on the GitHub repository.
